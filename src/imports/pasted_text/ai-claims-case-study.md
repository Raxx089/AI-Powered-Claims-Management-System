Project Name:
AI-Powered Claims Management Redesign

Industry:
Insurance / InsurTech

Product Type:
Enterprise B2B SaaS Platform with AI Integration

Goal:
Design a modern AI-powered claims management system that solves real-world problems insurance companies face globally.

Before creating the case study, analyze global insurance industry challenges using research insights and industry best practices.

Key global problems insurers face include:

• Manual claims processing
• Slow claims settlement
• Fraud detection challenges

• Document-heavy processes
• High operational costs
• Poor customer experience

Your task is to design a product solution that solves these problems through UX strategy, AI capabilities, and workflow automation.
