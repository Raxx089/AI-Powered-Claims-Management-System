Act as a Senior Product Designer, UX Researcher, and Product Strategist designing a B2B SaaS platform for the Insurance / InsurTech industry.

Your task is to design an AI-powered Claims Management System that solves real global problems insurance companies face in claims processing.

Context:
Insurance companies still rely on legacy systems and manual workflows for claims management. This causes slow processing, high operational costs, fraud risks, and poor customer experiences.

Global Problems to Solve:
• Claims processing is slow and manual
• Claims teams must review large volumes of documents
• Fraud detection is difficult and time consuming
• Information is scattered across multiple systems
• Claims adjusters lack clear insights and prioritization
• Communication between teams is inefficient
• Customers experience long claim settlement times

Your task is to design an intelligent SaaS product that simplifies the entire claims lifecycle using UX best practices and AI assistance.

Design a complete end-to-end experience including user flows, interface screens, and system interactions.

---

Target Users:

• Claims Adjusters
• Operations Managers
• Fraud Analysts
• Insurance Executives

---

Core Product Goal:

Design a platform that allows insurance companies to process claims faster, detect fraud earlier, automate repetitive tasks, and improve operational efficiency.

---

End-to-End User Flow to Design:

1. Claim Intake (First Notice of Loss – FNOL)
   Customer submits claim through digital form.

2. AI Claim Classification
   System automatically categorizes claim type and urgency.

3. Document Processing
   AI extracts data from uploaded documents using OCR.

4. Claims Dashboard
   Claims adjusters view prioritized claims in a central dashboard.

5. Claim Investigation
   Adjusters review claim details, supporting documents, and risk signals.

6. Fraud Detection
   AI flags suspicious claims using predictive risk scoring.

7. Collaboration
   Claims teams communicate internally to resolve issues.

8. Claim Approval Workflow
   Managers approve or reject claims based on insights.

9. Settlement
   Claim payment is processed and customer is notified.
